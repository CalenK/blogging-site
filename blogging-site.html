my growing intrest in tokusatsu

So this one will be shorter than the last one basically I've been gaining an intrest in japanese tokusatsu franchises, and for those who don't know what a tokusatsu is it's the japanese word for a piece of film that uses a lot of practical effects to bring the characters, monsters, and whatever else to life. You've most likely seen some toku through either old godzilla film dubs or power rangers and you never realized it before until now with what you just read, anways like I've mentioned in the about me I've only really gotten into three toku franchises so far but the three I am into are really good and interesting to check out so here's dome descriptions for said series.

  • Kamen Rider Kamen Rider is a series about masked motorcycle riding heroes that fight against evil human size and shaped monsters called kaijin, produced by toei the series has been around since 1971 and it continues to this day with the most recent entry at the time of writing this Kamen Rider My-th (pronouced as mice) having just started airing on japanese television and internationally on youtube. Each rider uses a device called a driver a belt they would wear around their waist, or in the two most recent entries across their upper body, outside of showa riders they would typically insert something in the buckle of the driver which would then activate their transformation (or henshin if you will) into their rider form. Ultimatly though I've been enjoying what I've seen of this franchise so far and I really do want and hope to see more of it
  • Power Rangers/Super sentai So to get this out of the way Sentai is a series also produced by toei that has been around since 1975 and is about a team of heroes that fight monsters that would be sent by an evil force or organization that would sometimes grow into a giant leading the team to need to pilot a giant robot in order for them to finish the job, saban would take footage and costume from sentai years later to create the Power Rangers in 1993. Now I did grew up with Power Rangers when I was a kid and I did recently watch a bit of KyuKyu Sentai gogov before shout factory lost the license to sentai but just note that while Power Rangers and Sentai are usally talk about/consinder two seperate franchises they are interlinked with each other in some way, and while both of them has recently ended with Sentai having it spirit lived on through project red and Power Rangers essentially well being on it's death bed at this point I still do like these franchises mainly one over the other considering that I've only watched on entry of the other but I still like them non the less.
  • Ultraman Ultraman is a toku Franchise produced by tsuburaya productions it started of as part of another series know as the ultra series which started with ultra q with the original ultraman originally airing in 1966. The premise of Ultraman is that there's someone who is usally apart of a science research organization that can become a giant with special powers to fight kaiju, much like Kamen Rider I've really been enjoying what I've seen of this franchise so far it's themes of humanity, heart, hope, and many others along with the kaiju action has really got me hooked on it and I want to see more of this franchise as well.
